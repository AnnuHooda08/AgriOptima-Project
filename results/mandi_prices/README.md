cleaning, monthly retraining, evaluation, graphing and model-saving workflow.

Both methods predict the same target months. The model wins the 2025 comparison,
but persistence wins validation. Do not call the linear model the validation
winner. The saved frozen-selection files document the earlier candidate audit;
its complete implementation and data hash input are in the external recovery copy.

November 2025 has only five reporting days. December uses that sparse November
price as an input. Both months fail the coverage screen; the original full-year
comparison remains visible. The 20-reporting-day screen does not prove all
mandis reported. Source composition changes in 2024 are another limitation.

predictions.csv includes each target, both predictions, training cutoff and
coverage flag. metrics.csv includes both periods and both coverage views.
monthly_data.csv contains the monthly inputs and coverage counts. Figures are
also embedded in the notebook. Every evaluation fit ends before its target month.
The research model's separate final fit ends in October 2025, before the gap.
It is not a current price service. No 2026 observations enter this experiment.
